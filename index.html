One shop, one street, three shelves

All-kits opened in 2019 with a rail of second-hand shirts and a notebook of sizes. Today the shop sells three kinds of jersey: the current home shirt, the retro shirt people ask for by season, and the printed shirt with a name and a number on the back.

The site exists because of one problem found in the old shop: stock and size were hidden until the checkout. Here both are visible before the cart — the size page shows the stock of the size you clicked, and the payment page shows the amount before you type a card number.
