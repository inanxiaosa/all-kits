Card declined? Open the payment help — the order is kept for 48 hours and nothing is charged twice.

Payment help. A declined card is usually a bank block on the first payment to a new shop. Try MobilePay, or call the bank and pay again with the same order number. The cart stays as it is until the order is paid or 48 hours pass.

What happens after the button

The confirmation names the delivery estimate — three to five days for standard, the next working day for pickup — and repeats the amount and the method. A printed shirt adds one day.
